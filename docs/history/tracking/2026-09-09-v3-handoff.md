## Working rules that bit this session

- Composed files must not be hand-edited; edit the layer and recompose. `build/` is gitignored.
- The additive Dissent rule and the required-sections rule are enforced at compose time and will refuse a sloppy port; that is intended.
- Reviewers and challengers cannot write files. Gate records and review documents are written by the coordinator from the returned message, and a reviewer's sentinel must be the last line of its file; put coordinator dispositions in a companion file.
- Session entry protocol applies: `git status` clean, board item exists (#138), ADR before architecture, tests before implementation. Spawn Wei for anything architectural and Vik + Tara for code; their reviews this session each found real defects the coordinator had missed.
